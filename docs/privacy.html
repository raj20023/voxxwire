Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

1. Introduction

At Voxxwire ("we", "our", or "us"), privacy is at the core of everything we build. Voxxwire is an offline, real-time speech translation desktop application for Windows. This Privacy Policy explains how our desktop application and website (voxxwire.com) process information.

2. Desktop Application Data Handling (100% Offline)

Voxxwire's desktop software is engineered to operate completely offline without sending data to external servers:

  • Audio Processing: Microphone and system audio streams are captured, processed with Voice Activity Detection (VAD), transcribed via Whisper ASR, and translated via Argos Translate entirely on your local CPU/GPU memory.
  • No Cloud Uploads: Audio recordings, transcripts, translated text, and spoken output are never transmitted over the internet, stored remotely, or shared with third parties.
  • Local Model Files: Language models and voice files are stored locally on your machine after installation.

3. Website Data Collection & Analytics

When you visit our website (voxxwire.com), standard technical information may be automatically logged by hosting infrastructure (GitHub Pages), such as your IP address, browser type, referring page, and access timestamp to ensure security and reliability.

4. Google AdSense & Cookies

We use Google AdSense to serve advertisements on our website. Google AdSense uses cookies and web beacons to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites on the internet:

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

5. Third-Party Links

Our website contains links to third-party repositories and services such as GitHub. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external sites.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you can reach out through our official GitHub repository issue tracker.
